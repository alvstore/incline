- Zoho direct OAuth secrets stay in service-role `zoho_direct_credentials` (via `zoho-oauth`); `settings.zoho_mode` is the kill switch and direct needs a passed test, so a broken login never silently stops sync.
- `access_logs.payload` stores scan metadata only, never camera frames or device tokens: every writer (mips-webhook-receiver, reconcile-mips-pass-records, any future importer) passes the raw event through `_shared/mipsPayload.ts#stripScanMedia` before insert, and `prune_access_log_media()` (called from `maintain_log_sizes()`) strips any oversized value older than a day — inline base64 made this one table 85% of the database and every feed load a multi-MB download.
- Member gate check-ins pass the hardware scan time (`member_check_in._at`, honoured only for internal callers, max 48h old, never future); never stamp attendance with arrival/poll time, because outage backlogs would pile up at reconnect time.
- Automation worker calls use a shared response deadline under waitUntil; transport failures stay unknown and are never immediately retried. Nurture limits AI candidates and stops starting new work at its elapsed budget, leaving remaining candidates for scheduled runs.
